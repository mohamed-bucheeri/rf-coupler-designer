About

Built by Mohamed Bucheeri, Communications and Networks Engineering, Bahrain Polytechnic. It started as a 10–30 MHz lumped Wilkinson divider whose textbook values failed at the top of the band, and grew into a tool that shows students why, and fixes it.

Use it as a starting point

Results come from circuit simulation of ideal and lossy models. Pads, junctions, connectors and part self-resonance are not modelled. Confirm the design in Qucs-S, Multisim or an EM simulator before you order boards or parts.
